## About

I am an Assistant Professor at ENTPE (University of Lyon & University Gustav Eiffel), specializing in learning-based optimization for transport and energy systems. My research focuses on:

- Graph Neural Networks
- Deep Learning for Transport Network Design
- Operations Research & Optimization
- Intelligent Transportation Systems
- Zero-Emission Mobility
